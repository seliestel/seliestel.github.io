# About this companion

These resources accompany **_Into Semiotics: Understanding Signs, Meaning, and Communication_**, an introductory book on the study of signs written by **Ignasi Ribó** and published by Mae Fah Luang University Learning Innovation Institute (Chiang Rai, Thailand, 2027). The book offers a concise but rigorous introduction to semiotics, aimed at graduate and undergraduate students, particularly in Thailand. It explains the foundational models of Saussure and Peirce, before moving on to cover key concepts in the study of communication, codes, cultural and applied semiotics, and the frontiers of semiotics beyond the human. This site translates that material into activities and exercises that students can *do*, either in a classroom setting or independently.

The activities included here make the concepts and ideas presented in the book more accessible and engaging. They rely on active-learning strategies: practical exercises, contextualized examples grounded in Thai realities, summary tables, real-life images, and technology-based tasks. The goal is to **help students move from abstract ideas to concrete application**, and to grasp difficult concepts by working with familiar material.

## Design principles

- **Static and lightweight.** The site is a plain [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/) build with no databases, no JavaScript, and no external dependencies. It is fast to load and is hosted on GitHub Pages.
- **Print-friendly.** Every chapter page can be printed or saved as a clean PDF worksheet with one click.
- **Bring-your-own-image.** Rather than using copyrighted photographs, activities ask students to capture or find their own images. This keeps the site small, avoids licensing problems, and provides opportunities for better pedagogy.
