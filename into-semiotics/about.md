# About this companion

These resources accompany **_Into Semiotics_**, an introductory textbook on the study of signs by **Ignasi Ribó** (Mae Fah Luang University, Chiang Rai, Thailand). The book offers a concise, rigorous introduction to semiotics — from the foundational models of Saussure and Peirce to communication, codes, cultural and applied semiotics, and the frontiers of semiotics beyond the human. This site translates that material into things students can *do*.

The activities were developed in response to a reviewer's suggestion that the text — while aimed at postgraduate students — would be more accessible and engaging with active-learning strategies: practical exercises, contextualized examples grounded in Thai realities, summary tables, real-life images, and technology-based tasks. The guiding goal is to **help students move from abstract ideas to concrete application**, and to grasp difficult concepts by working with familiar material.

## Design principles

- **Static and lightweight.** The site is a plain [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/) build with no databases, no build-time JavaScript, and no external dependencies — fast to load and cheap to host on GitHub Pages.
- **Easy to update.** Each chapter is a single Markdown file. Editing an activity means editing text.
- **Print-friendly.** Every chapter page can be printed or saved as a clean PDF worksheet with one click.
- **Bring-your-own-image.** Rather than shipping copyrighted photographs, activities ask students to capture or find their own images. This keeps the site small, avoids licensing problems, and is better pedagogy.
